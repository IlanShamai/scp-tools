Siman 94 — Ladle & Pot סימן צ״ד

Dairy ladle dipped into a meat pot. Interactive exploration of Shulchan Aruch vs. Rama on Ben Yomo, Kli Rishon, Kli Sheini, and Shishim — with material (metal/wood) and dipping frequency.

Siman 96 — Davar Charif סימן צ״ו

Sharp foods (onion, garlic, radish) cut with meat or dairy knives. When does the food become forbidden? S"A vs. Rama on Eino Ben Yomo, כדי נטילה, and knife condition.
