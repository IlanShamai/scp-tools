SECTION B
Dairy Spoon in Fleishig Bowl (Kli Sheini)
Introduction — What is Kli Sheini?
📄 SOURCE
(6) Ohel Yaakov (1) — most spoons today used in kli sheini, not kli rishon
📖 SA
Siman 105 Se'if 2 — S"A: kli sheini doesn't cook. Some: doesn't absorb. Third opinion: absorbs/expels up to kelipah. S"A: proper to follow third opinion
📄 SOURCE
Taz (105:4) — Maharshal: kli sheini can't do TWO actions (extract AND infuse), but CAN do one (extract); when it does, flavor fully absorbed
Application: Fleishig Spoon in Coffee
📄 SOURCE
According to S"A/Rama: coffee & mug permitted (bedieved). R' Akiva Eiger (105:2): spoon must be kashered. Darkei Teshuva (105:s): don't follow R' Akiva Eiger
📄 SOURCE
According to Maharshal: coffee forbidden (unless shishim); spoon needs kashering; mug ok
Dipping Dairy Spoon TWICE into Fleishig Pot
📖 SA
Se'if 2 — S"A: if dip twice WITHOUT realizing between = need 120x spoon (2x shishim). Rama: only need 1x shishim
📄 SOURCE
Shach (4) — S"A: first dip needs shishim; after removal spoon becomes chanan; second dip needs shishim against chanan
📄 SOURCE
Shach (5) — if realized after first dip: S"A says only one shishim (milk already batel)
📄 SOURCE
(10) Rav Shimon Shkop — why awareness matters: bitul is din in gavra (person), not cheftza (object)
📄 SOURCE
(9) Shulchan Aruch HaMevoar (p. 64) — Rama combines two leniencies: 1. No chanan for absorbed flavors in utensils. 2. Fusion only after removal (kli sheini)
SECTION C
Understanding and Applying Chanan
Basic Concept — What Everyone Agrees
📄 SOURCE
Chanan = חתיכה נעשית נבילה — when meat & milk mix without shishim, everything becomes forbidden. Need shishim against entire piece, and even with shishim must remove the piece
The Central Dispute
S"A PositionRama Position
Chanan ONLY for basar b'chalav (fusion of two permitted items) Chanan applies to ALL prohibitions
Application: Onion/Vegetable with Kosher Meat
📖 SA
Se'if 6 — S"A: onion with absorbed meat (cut with fleishig knife) or vegetable with meat goes into dairy pot = need shishim only against absorbed meat
📖 SA
Se'if 6 — Rama explains: no chanan for onion/vegetable (meat flavor permitted); chanan only for prohibitions
📄 SOURCE
(17) Be'er Heitev (17) from Shach (23) — when shishim against absorbed flavor, onion/vegetable itself permitted
📖 SA
Se'if 6 — Rama adds: same for pot walls — no chanan. Therefore: water in fleishig ben yomo pot doesn't become chanan; 24-hour count NOT restarted

TAKEAWAYS
Key Takeaways for Your Shiur

Essential Principles

  • Metal conducts heat (חם מקצתו חם כולו) — dispute whether this applies to flavor
  • Kli sheini doesn't cook/transfer — can extract but not extract AND infuse (Maharshal)
  • Chanan (חתיכה נעשית נבילה) — S"A: only basar b'chalav; Rama: all issurim
  • Chanan does NOT apply to permitted flavor (all agree) — onion with meat, water in pot

Practical Guidelines

  • Spoon in opposite pot: shishim against submerged portion (dispute: airspace vs. food)
  • Spoon in opposite bowl (kli sheini): bedieved permitted; consult Rav about Maharshal
  • Knife cutting opposite food: need shishim + remove kelipah (even today per most poskim)
  • Davar gush in kli sheini: follow Taz lechatchila; bedieved may rely on Rama
