SEPHASHBOTH
INTRO
Set the Stage — Before Opening Any Source
"If you had to choose a halacha in basar b'chalav where the extremes are on opposite ends — it's this. In a Sefardi home it's mutar l'chatchila; in an Ashkenazi home, mamash assur. Goal: learn the klal from the Gemara, get the two machlokos rishonim, see the S"A and Rama, then apply to 6–7 practical cases."
PART 1
The Gemara Sources
SOURCE 1
גמרא חולין קיא: — Open & Read
Radish (tzenon) cut with a ben-yomo fleishig knife → assur with dairy (kutach)
אגב חורפיה בלע — "because of its sharpness it absorbed." Rashi: sharpness in conjunction with the pressure of the knife together can remove flavor — even cold, without heat.
Context
The chiddush: Up until now, the only way to transfer flavor was through heat. This Gemara introduces, for the first time, that sharpness + pressure alone can transfer flavor cold. Ben-yomo knife only — that's what this Gemara talks about. What about eino ben-yomo? That's the next source.
SOURCE 2
משנה ע"ז לה: — Open & Read
Chaltis (mystery sharp vegetable) cut with a non-kosher eino ben-yomo knife → assur
אגב חורפיה מחלי לה לשמנוניתא והוה ליה כנותן טעם לשבח — the sharpness of the chaltis reawakens even a pagum (dormant, weakened) flavor, making it as if it's ben-yomo again.
Context
A second chiddush: Normally eino ben-yomo = get-out-of-jail-free card. Here, for the first time, sharpness overrides that. Even a knife sitting unused for a year in an Airbnb — you cut this vegetable with it, it's assur. Two questions now arise from these two Gemaros — these become the two fundamental machlokos rishonim.
PART 2
Two Machlokos Rishonim — Arising from the Gemaros
"Two questions from what we just saw. Question A: does the eino ben-yomo reawakening apply only to chaltis, or to all sharp foods? Question B: when a sharp food does absorb, how far does the flavor travel?"
SOURCE 3
בית יוסף — Machlokes A: Which foods reawaken eino ben-yomo?
Machlokes A — Lenient: מהר"ם, רבינו יחיאל
דוקא בקורט של חלתית — only chaltis can reawaken eino ben-yomo. All other sharp foods need a ben-yomo knife to absorb. ולא בשום דבר אחר חריף כלל. Many major poskim agree → lekula for everything else.
Context
The second opinion in the same source (Beis Yosef §3): רבינו ברוך says the opposite — all sharp foods (onions, garlic, leeks) have the same din as chaltis and can reawaken eino ben-yomo flavor. This becomes the Rama's position.
SOURCE 4
בית יוסף — Machlokes B: How far does flavor travel?
Machlokes B — Lenient: ראב"ד → flavor travels only כדי נטילה
The Gemara said assur — that means "big issur" = כדי נטילת מקום (about the width of a thumb, ~2–2.5 cm). Remove that layer and the rest is permitted.
Context
The Rashba pushes back: "If the Ra'avad hadn't already said it, I would say — the entire thing is assur. מפעפע בכולו — the sharpness pulls the flavor through the whole food." This becomes the Rama's position for Machlokes B as well.
"Now we have two machlokos sitting in front of us. Everyone agrees: ben-yomo knife + radish = fleishig. The dispute is (A) eino ben-yomo — does it matter? and (B) how far does it travel? Let's see how we pasken. Open the Shulchan Aruch."
PART 3
Pssak — S"A & Rama (סעיפים א׳–ג׳) — Interject Here
→ S"A
שולחן ערוך סעיף א׳ — Follows מהר"ם (A lenient) + ראב"ד (B lenient) SEPH
First question: ben yomo or not?
Eino ben-yomo knife → entirely mutar, no removal needed.
Ben-yomo knife → remove כדי נטילה (~2–2.5 cm from the cut surface) before using with opposite. Or taste — if no meat flavor, mutar b'di'avad (but ש"ך §5: don't rely on tasting l'chatchila).

"I go to Tzfas, knock on the S"A's door: 'Rabbi, I cut my onion with a dairy knife.' First question: ben yomo? Not ben yomo → go home. Ben yomo → remove k'dei netila. Shalom al Yisrael."
→ Rama
רמ"א סעיפים א׳ + ג׳ — Follows רבינו ברוך (A stringent) + רשב"א (B stringent) ASH
He won't even ask if it's ben yomo.
סעיף ג׳: Even an eino ben-yomo knife → food is assur (רבינו ברוך).
סעיף א׳: L'chatchila, flavor travels the entire food (רשב"א). B'di'avad (already in the pot — see ש"ך §10): only assur until כדי נטילה.

"I wake up the next morning, same thing, but now I'm in Krakow. I knock on the Rama's door. He's not going to ask me ben yomo or not. What does he tell me? Don't use that onion for fleishig. The whole thing becomes assur."
PART 4
Shishim Calculation — What if it's Already in the Pot?
"I forgot to remove k'dei netila and I put the onion in the cholent. The S"A says I need shishim against 'the part of the knife that touched.' What does that mean exactly?"
→ S"A
שולחן ערוך סעיף א׳ — Still inside the S"A
Need shishim against the smaller of: blade area that touched OR כדי נטילה
Key insight: Maximum flavor that can enter the k'dei netila = volume of the knife (not more, because no chanan by kosher knives).

Pocket knife (1 kezayis) + big onion (k'dei netila = 3 kezayis) → shishim against 1 kezayis.
Butcher's knife (5 kezayis) + small onion (k'dei netila = 3 kezayis) → shishim against 3 kezayis.
NOTE
Practical — removing k'dei netila: use a different knife type!
Use a fleishig or parve knife to remove the k'dei netila — not another dairy knife (that just creates another problem). Each side of a halved onion needs its own k'dei netila removed.
→ S"A
שולחן ערוך — Non-Kosher Knife: Same Rule SEPH
והוא הדין לכותכם בסכין של עובד כוכבים — no difference. Same shishim calculation: smaller of blade or k'dei netila. No chanan.
→ Rama
רמ"א + ש"ך §9 — Non-Kosher Knife: Chanan Changes Everything ASH
Diced with non-kosher knife: each cut = chanan → entire onion assur. Need shishim against the whole onion.
Cut in half, into pot: entire k'dei netila is assur via chanan — even if knife is smaller than k'dei netila.
B'di'avad logic: chanan applies up to where the flavor travels (k'dei netila), and within that space, chanan makes the whole k'dei netila assur.
PART 5
What Counts as Charif? — S"A סעיף ב׳ — Interject Here
→ S"A
שולחן ערוך סעיף ב׳ — like רמב"ם BOTH
All sharp foods = same din as radish
Garlic, onions, leeks (כרישין), other sharp vegetables, sour fruits (lemon in marinade), very salty fish (herring). Not limited to Gemara-listed sharps.
B'di'avad: the lenient opinion (only Gemara-listed sharps qualify) can serve as a צירוף for leniency.
PART 6
Pssak Lema'ase — Who Follows What
SOURCE 5
ילקוט יוסף (או"ה ח"ב עמ' יב) SEPH
Sephardim follow the S"A's stam — lekula
Two proofs: (1) Rule of סתם ויש: S"A's stam in סעיף א׳ = lenient view → we follow it. (2) In סעיף ג׳ the S"A doesn't even mention the eino ben-yomo yesh omrim → he rejected it entirely. Therefore: only ben-yomo knife activates absorption; flavor only reaches k'dei netila.
SOURCE 6
תשובות והנהגות ח"א-תלג ASH
Best practical advice: buy a set of parve knives
ראוי ונכון להשתמש לצנון ודברים חריפים בסכין פרווה דוקא — use a dedicated parve knife for all charif foods (onion, garlic, radish). Then you never have these shailos. A parve cutting board too.
PART 7
Three Kulas for Ashkenazim — Used in Combination
"Within the Rama it's very machmir. But there are three opinions that the Acharonim use. None by itself is enough — but in combination, there's room to be meikel."
SOURCE 7
אהל יעקב (סקס"ה) — בית מאיר · Kula 1
Eino ben-yomo kli + non-radish charif food → even Rama is meikel b'di'avad
Three reasons combined: (1) יש אומרים שאינו בן יומו מותר בדבר חריף — eino ben-yomo is mutar. (2) יש אומרים נ"ט בר נ"ט אף בדבר חריף — flavor goes down even with charif. (3) יש דעות שרק צנון הוא דבר חריף — only a radish is truly charif. All three together → even Rama meikel.
Context
Don't rely on Beis Meir alone. But when combined with the next two kulas, there's room in a tight situation.
NOTE
Kula 2 — פת"ש §4 · פנים מאירות
Cooking removes sharpness
Once a sharp food is cooked, it loses its charifness. So if the onion was already on the flame when it touched the opposite kli → may no longer be charif.
NOTE
Kula 3 — ערה"ש §20 (rejected by דרכי תשובה §20)
Oil removes sharpness immediately
The moment you add oil to the charif food, it loses its charifness — even before cooking. (Scientifically: oil coats the onion cells.) Used as a snif only.
PART 8
Practical Cases — The Rav's Order
"Now you have the klal. Let's go through the cases — we'll go to the S"A's house and then the Rama's house with each one."
Case A— Cut onion with dairy knife, then cut again with fleishig knife
→ S"A SEPH
First question: ben yomo? If either is eino ben yomo → mutar. If both ben yomo: were both cuts in the same k'dei netila? Yes → that k'dei netila is basar b'chalav, remove it.
→ Rama ASH
Whole onion is assur (basar b'chalav) l'chatchila. Already in pot → same shishim calculation as S"A. Most poskim won't rely on Beis Meir alone here. Ask LOR.
Case B— Cut onion with dairy knife, cooked in fleishig pan
→ S"A SEPH
Ben yomo? If one is eino ben yomo → mutar. If both ben yomo → onions and pan assur (pan needs kashering). No cooking doesn't help — heat transfers flavor without duchka.
→ Rama ASH
Assur regardless of ben yomo. Possible leniency if one is eino ben yomo: Beis Meir + ערה"ש (oil in pan). L'chatchila: don't rely on this unless mamash erev Shabbos / bein hashmashos. Ask LOR.
Case C— Dairy knife + dairy pan, mixed with fleishig spatula
→ S"A SEPH
Spatula ben yomo → heat transfers meat flavor → basar b'chalav. If no shishim → assur. Spatula eino ben yomo → mutar.
→ Rama ASH
Assur. If spatula eino ben yomo: combine Beis Meir + ערה"ש (oil) + עזר לשולחן עמ' קצא ("people stir after onions are already cooking = already lost sharpness"). Ask a Rav.
Case D— Dairy knife on fleishig cutting board (The Yeshiva Story)
→ S"A SEPH
At first glance: onion absorbs dairy (knife) + fleishig (board) = basar b'chalav. But: we don't view the onion's weight on the board as duchka d'sachina — that requires active cutting pressure.
→ Rama ASH
Also lenient b'di'avad. Source 9 — ספר דבר חריף פ"א-יג: Rav Elyashiv — lenient, even both ben yomo. חכמת אדם: machmir. Lema'ase: lenient.
SOURCE 10
שו"ע המבואר (תוספת הלכה עמ' קי) — Blender / Food Processor (Chanukah Latkes!)
מג"א: Cut onion with fleishig knife → blender → blender becomes fleishig
כתב המגן אברהם — you cut onion with a fleishig knife, then grind it in a crusher (= blender). The crusher absorbs meat flavor. Next time you run parve onions through it → they come out fleishig → can't eat latkes with sour cream.
Context
אבן העוזר argues (and this is how we pasken): The flavor the onion absorbed from the knife is weakened. לא שמענו שהוא חוזר להפליט מעצמו — we don't find that it can push out further into another kli. Many Acharonim (Chavos Daas, Rabi Akiva Eiger, Yeshuos Yaakov, Yad Yehuda) agree. Minhag lema'ase: follow the אבן העוזר. Blender stays parve. Also: dairy knife used on that same onion → dairy knife remains permitted (same reason).
Case E— Dairy/fleishig knife → parve pan; or parve pan + opposite spatula
→ S"A SEPH
Knife → parve pan: Pan stays parve (אבן העוזר — weakened flavor can't transfer further, even with heat).
Parve pan + fleishig spatula (ben yomo): Onions become fleishig. Pan stays parve.
→ Rama ASH
Same result on the pan — stays parve (דבר חריף פ"ט-ד). Spatula eino ben yomo → S"A: mutar; Rama: assur but room for leniency via ערה"ש + פת"ש. Ask a Rav.
SOURCE 11
ספר דבר חריף פ"א-י (based on רע"א) — Waiting after eating a fleishig onion?
No need to wait 6 hours after eating an onion cut with a ben-yomo fleishig knife
The two reasons to wait after meat: (1) meat taste in mouth, (2) meat stuck in teeth. Neither applies here — it's absorbed flavor in a vegetable, not actual meat. Similarly: after eating meat, you may eat an onion cut with a dairy knife.
PART 9
Rama's Addenda — Leaves & Store-Cut Produce
→ Rama
רמ"א — End of סעיף א׳
Leaves are not charif: Green stalks/leaves of radish or onion — even cut with a non-kosher knife, no removal needed (ערה"ש §17). The onion growing a tree in your drawer — cut the sprout with a dairy knife → fine for cholent.

Safek if non-kosher knife was used → mutar.

Store-cut radishes (bottom cut off): assume it was a field hoe → mutar.

City where all sharps are sold pre-cut (e.g. Dubai — only pre-sliced onions): buy them and remove k'dei netila. ט"ז §8: no other option = b'di'avad → even Rama lenient to require only k'dei netila.
